Clause 2: 25% Demo Calculation Settlement Guarantee

Following the 75% demo adjustment deduction, the account holder is entitled to claim the remaining 25% Demo Calculation Balance (₹62,50,000). Both Continue and Break / Deactivated accounts follow this exact 75% Demo Adjustment / 25% Demo Calculation formula.

Clause 3: 74-Day Demo Clearing & Withdrawal Cycle

Once the 25% Demo Calculation button is confirmed, the net payout of ₹62,50,000 enters the 74-Day Clearing & Escrow Settlement Pipeline. Funds remain visible under Pending Settlement with live milestone tracking until Day 74 simulated payout bank credit.
